Using the Bush Board

One shared page for the shopping list, packing list and who's paid for what. No app, no login, no password: just open the link.

The board
robruss055.github.io/balule-bush-board
First time opening it: if the shopping list looks empty, give it a few seconds to connect, then refresh the page once. After that it stays live and needs no more refreshing.
  1. 1

    Open it, pick your name

    Tap the link above. At the top of the page, tap your name — Cassy or Ashton. Do this once; the page remembers you.

    Your name is how the board knows which ticks and payments are yours, so everyone's totals stay correct.

  2. 2

    Shop tab

    Grouped by store, so it's a route: Checkers, then Food Lover's, then Makro, then Hoedspruit for ice. Tick an item when you buy it. That records that you paid for it, at its listed price.

    Bought something for a different price, or not on the list? Tap the item's name to correct the price or add who it's really for. To add something new, use the form at the bottom of the tab.

  3. 3

    Home tab

    Things we already have, so nobody buys them twice: coffee, spices, toilet roll, that sort of thing. Tick each one off once it's actually in the car.

  4. 4

    Pack tab

    What to pack, with the weather already worked in. Two kinds of item:

    for something personal, like a jacket — everyone ticks their own
    for something shared, like the cooler box — one tick for the unit
  5. 5

    Money tab

    Nothing to work out by hand. It shows what the whole trip costs, what each of us has paid, and who owes who, worked out automatically as items get ticked.

    Paid for fuel, or something in cash that isn't on the Shop list? Use Log a payment on this tab: say what it was for, the amount, who paid, and who it's split between.

  6. 6

    Trip tab

    Everything else in one place: expected weather, the route both ways, what Des is bringing, and what every unit is bringing.

Worth knowing

It's live for all three of us. The moment one of us ticks something, the others see it update — no need to say "I got the bread" in the group chat.

Works on any phone, no install. Bookmark the link or add it to your home screen so it's one tap away.

Nothing loading? Pull down or refresh the page once. This is only ever needed the very first time you open it on a device.

Questions go to Rob. Everything on the board updates for everyone, straight away.
