    The Trip

    Sunday 27 Sept

    What to expect

    Late September is the end of the dry season: warm, clear days and cool nights. The open game vehicle is the cold part. At 5 am with the wind it feels near 10°C, so a fleece and beanie are worth it even when the afternoon hits 30°C.

    • Sun: UV is high by 9 am. Hat, SPF 50, lip balm.
    • Mosquitoes: Balule is a low-risk malaria area. Use repellent and long sleeves from dusk. Ask a pharmacist if you want tablets.
    • Colours on drives: khaki, olive, brown and grey. Skip bright white and loud colours.
    • Rain: rare this month, but a thin windbreaker covers both wind and a stray shower.
